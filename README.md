## 🏙️ CleanCity
## 📖 Project Overview

CleanCity is a smart waste management web application designed to help citizens report waste-related issues in their local communities. The platform enables users to submit complaints by uploading images, adding descriptions, and specifying the location of the waste. These reports are sent to administrators, who can review, manage, and update their status. By connecting citizens with municipal authorities through a digital platform, CleanCity aims to promote cleaner neighborhoods and faster waste management.

## ❗ Problem Statement

Improper waste disposal and delayed waste collection are common challenges in many urban and rural areas. Citizens often have no simple way to report overflowing garbage bins, illegal dumping, or uncollected waste. As a result, complaints may be overlooked, leading to environmental pollution, unpleasant surroundings, and health risks.
